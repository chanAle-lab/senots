## Glacial & Periglacial Processes

- **Glacial erosional landforms:** Corries/cirques with tarns, arêtes, pyramidal peaks, U-shaped glacial troughs, hanging valleys, roche moutonnées (smooth plucked stoss-and-lee).
- **Glacial depositional landforms:** Moraines (terminal, lateral, medial, recessional), drumlins (streamlined elongate hills, steep blunt stoss end points up-ice), erratics, kettle holes.
- **Periglacial landforms:** Permafrost and active layer; solifluction/gelifluction sheets; patterned ground (stone polygons sorted by frost heave); ice wedges and fossil ice wedge pseudomorphs (prove continuous permafrost).
