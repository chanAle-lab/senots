## Ophiolite Complex (Oceanic Lithosphere Section)

- Complete preserved section of oceanic crust and upper mantle obducted onto land.
- 1. **Pelagic sediment:** Deep-sea chert, black mudstone, siliceous ooze (top).
- 2. **Pillow basalts:** Extrusive lavas quenched in seawater.
- 3. **Sheeted dyke complex:** Sub-vertical feeder dykes formed in 100% extensional spreading zone.
- 4. **Gabbro:** Coarse-grained, isotropic at top, layered cumulate at base.
- 5. **Moho Discontinuity:** Petrographic boundary between gabbro and peridotite.
- 6. **Ultramafic mantle:** Harzburgite / lherzolite, often altered to serpentinite.
