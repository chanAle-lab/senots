## Terranes & Orogenic Cycles of the British Isles

- **Caledonian Orogeny (Ordovician–Devonian):** Closure of Iapetus Ocean; Laurentia (Scotland & NW Ireland) collided with Eastern Avalonia (England & Wales). Marked by the Iapetus Suture zone.
- **Variscan Orogeny (Carboniferous–Permian):** Closure of Rheic Ocean; collision of Armorica/Gondwana with Euramerica to assemble Pangaea. Intense folding in SW England (Cornubian batholith emplacement).
- **Northward Drift of Britain:**
- • Cambrian: ~60°S (cold seas).
- • Carboniferous: Equatorial ~0° (warm tropical coal-forming swamps).
- • Permian–Triassic: 10°–20°N (arid trade wind desert, New Red Sandstone).
- • Present: ~54°N (temperate).
