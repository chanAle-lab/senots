# Topic G1.2 · Metamorphic Rocks: Mineralogy & Texture
**Specification Section:** Interpreting the Geological Record  
**Key Topics:** Metamorphic facies, P-T-t paths, microstructures, porphyroblasts, shear zones  

---

## Metamorphic Facies & Tectonic Settings

- **Zeolite & Prehnite-Pumpellyite:** Low P, low T; burial metamorphism.
- **Greenschist:** Moderate P, moderate T (300–450°C). Key minerals: Chlorite, actinolite, albite, epidote.
- **Amphibolite:** Moderate-high P, high T (500–700°C). Key minerals: Hornblende, plagioclase, garnet, staurolite.
- **Granulite:** High T (>750°C), anhydrous, deep continental crust. Key minerals: Pyroxene, sillimanite, garnet, perthitic feldspar.
- **Blueschist:** High P, low T. Subducting slab at ocean trenches. Key diagnostic mineral: **Glaucophane** (blue sodic amphibole).
- **Eclogite:** Ultra-high P, high T. Deeply subducted crust. Key minerals: **Omphacite** (green sodic pyroxene) + **Pyrope garnet** (pink).

## Microtextures & Shear-Sense Indicators

- **Porphyroblastic & Poikiloblastic:** Large metamorphic crystals (e.g. garnet, staurolite, andalusite) containing inclusions of earlier matrix minerals (sieve texture).
- **Helicitic / Snowball Garnet:** Sigmoidal spiral trails of quartz inclusions inside garnet prove crystal rotated during ductile shear (syn-tectonic growth).
- **Mylonites:** Intense ductile grain-size reduction in shear zones. Features: Ribbon quartz, dynamically recrystallised mortar texture.
- **Rotated Porphyroclasts:**
- • *Sigma (\(\sigma\)) clasts:* Tails / wings extend symmetrically into foliation; indicate shear sense.
- • *Delta (\(\delta\)) clasts:* Tails curved and wrapped around porphyroclast from significant rotation.
