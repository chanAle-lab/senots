## Geophysical Exploration Methods

- **Gravity survey (Gravimeter):** Measures local variations in gravitational field (\(g\)).
- • *Positive anomaly (gravity high):* Dense subsurface bodies (massive metallic sulfide ore bodies, mafic/ultramafic intrusions).
- • *Negative anomaly (gravity low):* Low-density bodies (salt domes, granite plutons, sedimentary basins, caverns).
- **Magnetic survey (Magnetometer):** Detects variations in Earth's magnetic field due to magnetic minerals (magnetite, pyrrhotite).
- **Seismic reflection:** Generates acoustic waves; reflected at boundaries between rocks of differing **acoustic impedance (\(Z = ho 	imes v\))**. Produces cross-sectional profiles of subsurface structures and traps.

## Geochemical Prospecting & Borehole Logging

- **Geochemical survey:** Systematically samples soil, stream sediments, or water to identify anomalous concentrations above regional background levels.
- • Contour maps plotted with isolines (e.g. 50 ppm, 100 ppm Cu); highest values pinpoint upstream or upslope ore subcrop.
- **Borehole geophysical logging:**
- • *Gamma log:* Measures natural radioactivity. High gamma = shale/clay (rich in K-40); Low gamma = sandstone, limestone, evaporites.
- • *Resistivity log:* High resistivity = hydrocarbons or fresh water; Low resistivity = saline formation water in pores.
- • *Sonic log:* Measures seismic velocity to calculate porosity.
