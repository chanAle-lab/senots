# Topic G3.3 · Evidence for Climate Change
**Specification Section:** Interpreting the Geological Record  
**Key Topics:** Oxygen isotope proxies (d18O), ice cores, sedimentary paleoclimate indicators  

---

## Oxygen Isotopes (d18O) as Paleothermometers

- 16O (light) evaporates preferentially over 18O (heavy).
- **During Glacial / Icehouse Periods:**
- • Light 16O evaporates, falls as snow, and is trapped in continental ice sheets.
- • **Ice cores have negative \(\delta^{18}	ext{O}\)** (depleted in 18O).
- • **Oceans become enriched in 18O -> marine foraminifera shells have positive \(\delta^{18}	ext{O}\)**.
- **During Interglacial Periods:** Melting ice returns 16O to oceans; marine carbonate \(\delta^{18}	ext{O}\) decreases.

## Sedimentary & Biological Climate Indicators

- **Glacial:** Tillites / diamictites (unsorted, striated clasts), dropstones deforming laminations in marine shale.
- **Arid / Desert:** Aeolian dunes (large-scale cross-bedding), red beds (hematite coating), evaporites (halite, gypsum).
- **Humid / Tropical:** Thick coal beds (warm swamps), laterites/bauxites (leached tropical soils).
- **Foraminiferal coiling:** *Neogloboquadrina pachyderma* coils to the left (sinistral) in polar water (<7°C) and to the right (dextral) in warmer waters.
