## Magma Compositions & Igneous Classification

- **Basaltic / Mafic (45–52% SiO2):** Low viscosity, high temp (1000–1200°C), dense, low gas. Minerals: Ca-rich plagioclase, augite, olivine. Extrusive: Basalt. Intrusive: Gabbro / Dolerite.
- **Andesitic / Intermediate (52–66% SiO2):** Moderate viscosity & temp (800–1000°C). Minerals: Na-Ca plagioclase, hornblende, biotite. Extrusive: Andesite. Intrusive: Diorite.
- **Rhyolitic / Silicic / Felsic (>66% SiO2):** High viscosity, lower temp (700–900°C), low density, volatile-rich (explosive). Minerals: Quartz, orthoclase, Na-plagioclase, muscovite/biotite. Extrusive: Rhyolite. Intrusive: Granite.

## Igneous Textures & Cooling Rates

- **Equigranular / Phaneritic:** Coarse crystals (>1 mm), uniform slow cooling deep underground (e.g. granite, gabbro).
- **Aphanitic:** Fine crystals (<1 mm), rapid cooling at or near surface (e.g. basalt, rhyolite).
- **Porphyritic:** Two distinct crystal sizes: large *phenocrysts* formed during early slow cooling at depth, set in fine *groundmass* formed during rapid cooling upon ascent/eruption.
- **Glassy (Obsidian):** Instantaneous quenching, no time for crystal lattice to form.
- **Vesicular & Amygdaloidal:** Gas bubbles trapped upon eruption (vesicles e.g. pumice, scoria); later filled by secondary minerals like calcite or quartz (amygdales).

## Intrusions vs Extrusions (Field Recognition)

- **Concordant intrusions:** Parallel to bedding. *Sill:* Chilled margins top and bottom; baked margins top and bottom; country rock xenoliths; no vesicles.
- **Extrusive lava flow:** Chilled margin only at base; baked margin only below; vesicular, weathered/eroded top; fragments incorporated into overlying sedimentary bed.
- **Discordant intrusions:** Cut across country rock. *Dyke* (steep tabular sheet), *Pluton / Batholith* (large discordant magma chamber >100 km²).

## Contact (Thermal) Metamorphism

- High temperature, low pressure, isotropic (uniform) confining stress. Occurs in **metamorphic aureole** surrounding igneous intrusions.
- **Aureole zones in pelitic (shale/mudstone) host rock:**
- • Outer zone (lowest temp): *Spotted slate / Spotted rock* (incipient growth of biotite/cordierite clusters).
- • Middle zone: *Andalusite hornfels* (porphyroblasts of chiastolite with carbonaceous cross inclusions).
- • Inner zone (highest temp): *Sillimanite-cordierite hornfels* (dense, hard, granoblastic, non-foliated).
- Pure rocks: Limestone -> Marble (coarse granoblastic calcite); Sandstone -> Metaquartzite (interlocking quartz grains).

## Regional Metamorphism & Foliation Sequence

- High directed stress + high confining pressure + elevated temperature. Convergent plate collisions / orogenesis.
- **Barrovian prograde metamorphic zones (pelite sequence):**
- 1. **Slate:** Microscopic platy minerals aligned perpendicular to maximum principal stress (\(\sigma_1\)) -> *slaty cleavage*.
- 2. **Phyllite:** Fine white mica/chlorite gives a silky lustrous sheen; wavy foliation.
- 3. **Schist:** Medium-coarse visible platy minerals (muscovite/biotite) forming continuous foliation (*schistosity*); contains porphyroblasts (garnet, staurolite).
- 4. **Gneiss:** High-grade segregation into alternating light quartzo-feldspathic bands and dark mafic (biotite/hornblende) bands (*gneissose banding*).
- 5. **Migmatite:** Partial melting of felsic components (anatexis) forming mixed metamorphic-igneous rock.
