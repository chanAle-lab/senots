## Three-Point Problems & Strike Lines

- **Strike lines (structural contours):** Straight lines connecting points of equal elevation on a planar geological surface (bedding plane or fault).
- • The direction of a strike line is the **strike** of the bed.
- • Spacing between strike lines indicates dip: closer lines = steeper dip; wider lines = gentler dip.
