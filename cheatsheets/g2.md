# Topic G2 · Rock Deformation & Structures
**Specification Section:** Interpreting the Geological Record  
**Key Topics:** Stress/strain ellipsoids, Flinn diagrams, deformation mechanisms, stereonets  

---

## Stress & Strain Ellipsoids

- **Stress:** Force applied per unit area. Three mutually perpendicular principal stress axes: \(\sigma_1\) (maximum), \(\sigma_2\) (intermediate), \(\sigma_3\) (minimum).
- **Strain:** Deformation/distortion of rock body resulting from applied stress. Strain ellipsoid axes: \(X \ge Y \ge Z\).
- **Flinn Diagram:** Plots strain ratio \(a = X/Y\) vs \(b = Y/Z\).
- • \(k > 1\): Constrictional strain (prolate 'cigar' shape, e.g. pencil cleavage).
- • \(k = 1\): Plane strain (no volume change in Y).
- • \(k < 1\): Flattening strain (oblate 'pancake' shape, e.g. slaty cleavage).

## Deformation Mechanisms & Stereonets

- **Pressure solution:** Dissolution of minerals at high-stress grain contacts and precipitation in low-stress shadows (forms stylolites in limestones).
- **Dislocation creep / Plastic glide:** Movement of crystal lattice imperfections; dynamic recrystallisation.
- **Stereonet interpretation:** Poles to bedding planes plotted on equal-area stereonet lie along a great circle (the \(\pi\)-girdle); the pole to this girdle defines the **trend and plunge of the fold axis**.
