# Topic G1.1 · Magma Generation & Evolution
**Specification Section:** Interpreting the Geological Record  
**Key Topics:** Partial melting, magmatic differentiation, phase diagrams, crystallization paths

## Magma Generation Mechanisms

- **Decompression melting:** Upward movement of solid mantle peridotite without loss of heat (adiabatic) crosses solidus. Occurs at mid-ocean ridges and hotspots.
- **Flux melting (hydration):** Subducting oceanic slab dewaters (serpentinite/clays break down); water enters overlying mantle wedge, lowering solidus temperature below ambient temperature. Occurs at subduction zones.
- **Thermal melting:** Conductive heat transfer from hot basaltic magma underplating crust melts continental crust.

## Magmatic Differentiation Processes

- **Fractional crystallisation / Crystal settling:** Early-formed dense mafic minerals (olivine, Ca-plagioclase, pyroxene) settle out of liquid under gravity, depleting magma in Mg, Fe, Ca and enriching remaining liquid in Si, Na, K.
- **Crustal assimilation:** Hot rising magma melts and incorporates wall rock (xenolith digestion), shifting magma towards more silicic composition.
- **Magma mixing:** Influx of primitive mafic magma into evolving silicic chamber creates intermediate hybrids with mixed phenocrysts (e.g. resorbed quartz beside calcic plagioclase).
- **Liquid immiscibility:** Magma splits into two unmixable liquids (e.g. sulfide-rich melt and silicate-rich melt).

## Phase Diagrams & The Lever Rule

- **Binary Eutectic System (e.g. Diopside-Anorthite):**
- • *Liquidus:* Curve above which system is completely liquid.
- • *Solidus:* Line below which system is completely solid.
- • *Eutectic point:* Minimum melting temperature where liquid and both solid phases coexist at equilibrium. Final liquid always crystallises at eutectic composition.
