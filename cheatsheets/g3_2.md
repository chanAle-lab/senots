# Topic G3.2 · Factors in Climate Change
**Specification Section:** Interpreting the Geological Record  
**Key Topics:** Milankovitch cycles, greenhouse gases, plate tectonics, oceanic circulation  

---

## Milankovitch Astronomical Cycles

- **Orbital Eccentricity (100,000 & 400,000 yr):** Earth's orbit cycles between circular and elliptical, altering total incoming solar radiation.
- **Obliquity / Axial Tilt (41,000 yr):** Tilt cycles between 22.1° and 24.5° (currently 23.4°). Greater tilt = more extreme seasons (warmer summers, colder winters). Less tilt favours ice sheet growth as mild summers fail to melt winter snow.
- **Precession of Equinoxes (21,000–26,000 yr):** Wobble of Earth's rotational axis; alters timing of seasons relative to perihelion.

## Tectonic & Greenhouse Controls

- **Continental position:** Landmasses over poles allow ice sheet buildup (e.g. Antarctica in Cenozoic).
- **Oceanic gateways:** Opening of Drake Passage separated South America from Antarctica -> Antarctic Circumpolar Current thermally isolated Antarctica, initiating continental glaciation (~34 Ma).
- **Mountain uplift & CO2 drawdown:** Himalayan collision exposed vast silicate rocks; intense chemical weathering (hydrolysis) drew down atmospheric CO2, driving global Cenozoic cooling.
