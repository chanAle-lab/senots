## The Petroleum System (Five Essentials)

- 1. **Source Rock:** Organic-rich (sapropelic) marine mudstone/shale with >1% Total Organic Carbon (TOC). Plankton buried in anoxic conditions.
- 2. **Maturation:** Thermal cooking under burial:
- • *Oil Window:* 60°C to 120°C (burial depth 2–4 km).
- • *Gas Window:* 120°C to 200°C (burial depth 4–6 km).
- • Above 200°C: Over-mature; hydrocarbons graphitised/destroyed.
- 3. **Reservoir Rock:** Porous (stores fluids) and permeable (allows extraction). E.g. well-sorted sandstone, oolitic/reefal limestone.
- 4. **Cap Rock (Seal):** Impermeable rock barrier preventing escape. E.g. thick shale, mudstone, or evaporites (halite/anhydrite).
- 5. **Trap:** Geometric arrangement halting migration:
- • *Structural:* Anticlinal crest, fault trap (reservoir juxtaposed against impermeable shale), salt dome/diapir.
- • *Stratigraphic:* Unconformity trap, sand pinch-out / lens, reef buildup.

- **Permeability:** Interconnectedness of pores allowing fluid flow.
- **Coalification Rank:** Progressive increase in burial T, P, and time:
- • Peat -> Lignite (brown coal) -> Bituminous coal (lustrous, black) -> Anthracite (hard, shiny, non-smudge).
- • Trend: Carbon content increases (up to 95%), calorific value increases, moisture and volatile content decrease.
