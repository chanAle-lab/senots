## Evolutionary Mechanisms & Patterns

- **Phyletic gradualism:** Slow, steady, continuous transformation of lineages over geological time.
- **Punctuated equilibrium:** Long periods of morphological stasis interrupted by rapid speciation events in small isolated populations.
- **Adaptive radiation:** Rapid diversification of an ancestral group into many ecological niches following evolutionary novelty or mass extinction opening up vacant ecospace.

## The Big Five Mass Extinctions

- **1. End-Ordovician (~444 Ma):** Glaciation on Gondwana, eustatic sea-level drop, loss of shallow marine shelf habitat.
- **2. Late Devonian (~375 Ma):** Oceanic anoxia, cooling; hit shallow benthic corals/brachiopods.
- **3. End-Permian ('The Great Dying', ~252 Ma):** Largest extinction in history (~96% marine species, ~70% terrestrial vertebrates lost).
- • *Causes:* **Siberian Traps flood basalt volcanism** releasing massive CO2 and SO2 -> global warming, ocean acidification, widespread marine anoxia, ozone layer destruction.
- **4. End-Triassic (~201 Ma):** Central Atlantic Magmatic Province (CAMP) rifting; paved way for dinosaur dominance.
- **5. End-Cretaceous (K-Pg, ~66 Ma):** Non-avian dinosaurs, ammonites, marine reptiles extinct.
- • *Causes:* **Chicxulub bolide impact** (Iridium anomaly in boundary clay, shocked quartz, tektites/microkrystites, tsunami deposits) combined with **Deccan Traps volcanism**.
