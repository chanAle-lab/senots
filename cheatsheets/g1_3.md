## Bouma Sequence (Turbidite Deposition)

- Formed by decelerating subaqueous density flow in deep marine environment.
- **Ta division:** Massive to normally graded coarse sandstone/gravel, sharp erosive base with sole marks (flute casts, groove casts).
- **Tb division:** Parallel plane-laminated sandstone (upper flow regime flat bed).
- **Tc division:** Ripple cross-laminated or climbing ripple sandstone/siltstone with convolute lamination (lower flow regime).
- **Td division:** Parallel laminated siltstone.
- **Te division:** Structureless hemipelagic clay/mud.

## Paleocurrent Vector Mean & Distributions

- **Paleocurrent measurements:** Flute casts, cross-bed dips, ripple crests, parting lineations.
- • Unimodal distribution (rose diagram with one strong direction): Fluvial / river channel or delta.
- • Bimodal / bipolar distribution (two opposite peaks 180° apart): Tidal marine shelf (flood and ebb tides).
- • Polymodal / random: Deep ocean pelagic or aeolian variable dune.
