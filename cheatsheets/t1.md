## Seismic Hazards & Volcanic Monitoring

- **Earthquake hazards:** Ground shaking, liquefaction (water-saturated unconsolidated sand behaves as liquid), landslides, tsunamis.
- **Volcanic prediction precursors:**
- • Harmonic volcanic tremor and shallow earthquake swarms (magma moving through conduits).
- • Ground deformation: Tiltmeters, GPS, and satellite InSAR detect swelling / inflation of volcanic edifice.
- • Gas emissions: Increasing SO2 and CO2 emission rates and ratios.
- • Thermal anomalies: FLIR infrared imaging detects rising magma.

## Slope Stability & Engineering Geology

- **Factor of Safety (\(F\)):** \(F = rac{\sum 	ext{Resisting Forces}}{\sum 	ext{Driving Forces}}\). If \(F > 1\) slope is stable; \(F < 1\) failure occurs.
- • Driving forces: Gravity component parallel to slope, slope surcharge (buildings).
- • Resisting forces: Cohesion, friction across potential slip planes.
- • **Pore water pressure:** Reduces effective normal stress across slip planes, triggering landslides.
- **Engineering mitigation:** Horizontal drainage pipes (relieves pore pressure), rock bolts (pins beds together across dip), retaining walls with weep holes, gabion baskets, shotcrete.
